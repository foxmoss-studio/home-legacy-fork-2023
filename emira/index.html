Emira

Your life is more than a list of things to get done.

Emira is a life calendar where you can set goals, keep memories, journal, and play little relaxing games.

It was made as much for reflection as planning. There are no streaks to maintain, no overdue goals, and no productivity score telling you how well you are living your life.

What it does

Keep some of your life close.

See your life

The Life Calendar lays your life out in weeks, months, and years. You choose the life-expectancy frame yourself. It gives the calendar a shape without turning your future into a countdown.

Keep a few things close

Eventually can hold as many someday ideas as you want. Monthly Goals stay intentionally small: up to three at once, including zero. Goals can change, return to Eventually, be completed, or be released without penalties.

Remember what mattered

Milestones keep meaningful moments, including ordinary memories, not only achievements. Add dates, descriptions, photos, and links to Journal Pages when the pieces belong together.

Keep a journal

Journal Pages can cover a day or a range of days, hold photos and styled writing, and be reread as a book. There is no empty-day calendar waiting to make you feel behind.

Leave something for later

Time Capsules let you leave a message and optional photo for your future self. Reflection Reviews occasionally invite you to look back at what happened, not audit whether you completed enough goals.

Take a quiet minute

Six activities live outside the planning loop: Breathe, Present, Echo, Restore, Memory, and Call. They can ask for attention or coordination without turning into ranks, streaks, or life points.

Why it feels different

No pressure attached.

Some months you might have three goals. Some months you might have none. Emira does not create an overdue state, a catch-up queue, or a score for the parts of life you happened to record.

Not every time you open Emira needs a purpose. You can sit with the Life Calendar, look through old pages, listen to ambient music, change the colors, play something for a minute, or close the app again. Nothing is waiting to scold you.

Emira also does not use AI to analyze your Journal, goals, memories, photos, or mood. Its words are written on purpose, but it does not pretend those words mean it knows what you are feeling.

Make it yours

A place you can actually want to come back to.

Color and appearance

Color profiles, app icons, backgrounds, paper choices, and other appearance controls change the atmosphere while keeping the meaning of the interface stable.

Sound and motion

Ambient music, small interaction sounds, haptics, and slow backgrounds are part of the feel. Reduced Motion keeps movement from being required to understand or use Emira.

Use less of it

Life, Goals, Milestones, Journal, and Games can be hidden from your normal view if they are not part of how you use Emira. Hiding a section does not mean deleting its saved content.

Your own devices

Emira works locally by default. Optional private iCloud sync can keep your own supported Apple devices in step, and backup/export tools keep your library from being trapped in one installation.

Access

Free to download. Annual when you want to create.

Eligible new subscribers can try Emira Annual free for one week. The App Store shows the current price for your storefront before purchase. An active subscription lets you create and edit Emira content.

If your subscription ends, the things you already saved remain available to read and export. Ending access does not erase your library. Purchase, trial eligibility, billing, and subscription management are handled by Apple.

Frequently asked questions

A few things people reasonably ask.

Life Calendar and goals
Is the Life Calendar a countdown to death?

No. It is a visual frame for seeing the life you have lived at different scales. You choose the life-expectancy number yourself. It is not a medical estimate, prediction, or giant days-remaining timer.

Why only three Monthly Goals?

Because a giant active goal list stops feeling useful quickly. Emira lets you keep up to three things close for the month while unlimited someday ideas can wait in Eventually.

What happens if I do not finish one?

Nothing bad. There is no overdue state or failure badge. Keep it, change it, return it to Eventually, complete it later, or release it.

Can I have zero goals?

Yes. Zero is a normal number of goals.

Journal, Milestones, and memories
Do I have to journal every day?

No. Journal shows the pages you actually make. It does not create a calendar full of empty days for you to catch up on.

Can a Journal Page cover more than one day?

Yes. A page can represent a day or a range of days, which is useful for a weekend, trip, week, or another stretch of life that belongs together.

Does a Milestone have to be an achievement?

No. A Milestone can be important, funny, ordinary, personal, or simply something you want to be able to find again later.

Can Journal Pages and Milestones connect?

Yes. They can refer to the same meaningful event without forcing the memory into only one form.

Games and quiet activities
Why does a life app have games?

Because not every useful minute in an app needs to be planning or writing. Breathe, Present, Echo, Restore, Memory, and Call are small places to put your attention somewhere else for a bit.

Are they competitive?

No. They are not built around leaderboards, streaks, lives, or turning play into a productivity system.

Do games unlock goals or rewards?

No. Playing does not earn points for your life or prove you had a productive day.

Privacy and your data
Does Emira send my Journal to an AI service?

No. There is no AI feature analyzing your Journal, goals, memories, photos, or mood.

Do I need an Emira account?

No. Emira works without its own account system. Optional iCloud uses the Apple Account on your device.

Does Emira need my whole photo library?

No. Photo and camera access is used when you choose an action that needs it, and Apple system controls let you manage that permission.

What happens to my data if I stop subscribing?

Your existing saved content remains available to read and export. Ending a subscription does not delete your library.

Platform and accessibility
What does Emira run on?

Emira is a native iPhone and iPad app with an Apple Watch companion. The current native app targets iOS 17 or later.

Does Emira support Reduced Motion?

Yes. Motion is part of the atmosphere, not a requirement for understanding or operating the app.

Does Emira work with VoiceOver?

Accessibility is part of the app's design and ongoing validation, including meaningful labels and equivalent controls where a visual gesture alone would not be enough.

Is Emira public yet?

Not yet. This page will link to the actual App Store listing after public availability is confirmed.
